# Record Templates

The manual includes four example record sheets (Manual pp. 24–27): visitor record, materials import inspection record, vehicle inspection record and crop monitoring record. Those pages didn't survive text extraction, so the layouts below are rebuilt from what the manual says each record must capture elsewhere in the text. Adapt column names to the user's operation; they are not the official forms.

## 1. Visitor register
Purpose: track people movement and manage safety (Manual p. 14).

| Date | Name | Company / organisation | Phone | Purpose of visit | Areas visited | Visited another nursery/farm or overseas recently? (when, where) | Footwear/clothing clean on arrival (Y/N) | Cleaning provided (boot wash, covers, overalls) | Time in | Time out | Host | Biosecurity briefing given (Y/N) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 2. Materials import (production inputs) register / inspection record
Purpose: trace-back of every input (Manual pp. 6, 12).

| Date received | Item (media, containers, fertiliser, propagation material, plants) | Supplier and contact details | Supplier accreditation (NIASA / BioSecure HACCP / none) | Batch / lot | Quantity | Source, treatment and testing history provided (Y/N, details) | Inspection result (pests, soil, plant debris, damage) | Action taken | Storage or planting location | Date used | Inspected by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 3. Vehicle and machinery inspection record
Purpose: record inspection of every vehicle entering, and wash-down outcomes (Manual pp. 17–18).

| Date | Vehicle / equipment (rego or ID) | Owner / driver / company | Coming from (last property or region) | Inspected areas (tyres, floor mats, trays, undercarriage, internal spaces) | Soil / plant material / pests found | Washed down (Y/N, where) | Disinfectant used | Re-inspected and cleared by | Entry allowed to (parking only / storage / production) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 4. Crop monitoring record
Purpose: record presence, absence and levels of established and exotic pests; supports market "evidence of absence" (Manual p. 10). Add priority exotic pests to the sheet as rows to check every time.

| Date | Block / bench / house | Crop and stage | Plants inspected (number) | Monitoring method (visual, sticky traps, beat sampling, indicator plants) | Pest or symptom | Level (count, % plants affected) | Priority exotic pests checked (list) and result (absent/suspect) | Photo taken (Y/N, file ref) | Action taken / reported to | Monitored by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 5. Spray diary (pesticide application record)
The manual says a spray diary should accompany each consignment of plant material (Manual p. 13). Typical fields: date, block/crop, target pest, product, active ingredient, rate, volume applied, method, operator, withholding/re-entry period, consignment(s) it applies to. Follow local legal record-keeping requirements.

## 6. Suspect exotic pest incident log
Built from the manual's response steps (Manual p. 11): date/time found, who found it, exact location (marked Y/N), plant(s) and symptoms, photos, access restricted (Y/N), hygiene done (hands, clothes, boots), hotline called (time, reference, officer name), sample instructions received, operations restricted until, outcome.
